FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q: How do I track my shipment?
A: Use the Shipment ID.

Q: What causes shipment delays?
A: Weather, supplier issues, warehouse congestion, and traffic.

Q: Can I change the delivery address?
A: Yes, before dispatch.

Q: What if my shipment is delayed?
A: The customer will receive a notification.

Q: How can I contact support?
