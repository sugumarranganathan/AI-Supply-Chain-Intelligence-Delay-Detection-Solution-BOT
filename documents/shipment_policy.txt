SHIPMENT POLICY

Shipment ID Format:
- SHP1001
- SHP1002
- SHP1003

Shipment Status:
- Pending
- Processing
- In Transit
- Delayed
- Delivered

Delay Rules:
- Heavy rain may delay shipments by 24 hours.
- Floods may delay shipments by 48 hours.
- Mechanical failures may delay shipments by 12 hours.
- Customs clearance may delay international shipments.

Priority Shipments:
High-priority shipments should be processed first.

Customer Notification:
Customers should receive updates whenever shipment status changes.
